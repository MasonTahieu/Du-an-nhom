CAC FILE DUOC CAP NHAT
- scripts/Start.ps1: ep tien trinh Python chay ngam ghi log bang UTF-8.

- static/index.html, static/style.css: giu khung nap du lieu co the thu gon/mo rong.

HUONG DAN TREN WINDOWS
1. Dong khung nap va DOI cho job hien tai ket thuc neu con dang nap.
2. Chay Tat-Ung-Dung.cmd (hoac dung Ctrl+C neu chay server tu cua so CMD).
3. SAO LUU thu muc du an hien tai (vi du D:\Du an 5 chieu).
4. Giai nen TOAN BO ban va ZIP vao DUNG thu muc goc dang co
   Mo-Ung-Dung.cmd, static/, scripts/, vnresearch/.
5. Khi WinRAR hoi ghi de, chon Yes to All.
6. Chay lai Mo-Ung-Dung.cmd; bam Ctrl+F5 trong trinh duyet.
7. Mo khung 'Nap du lieu theo ma', nhap VND, tich bo sung BCTC quy/vi mo/tin,
   bam Nap/cap nhat ma nay de thu lai.
